Positioning for the forklift maintenance tool. Target is warehouse operations managers at
single-site distribution centres running 8 to 40 forklifts.

Here's my value proposition grid. Short, because honestly there's nothing to compare against — we
have no competitors. There's no product that does preventive maintenance scheduling for forklift
fleets at this size. The big enterprise asset-management suites start at 200 vehicles and cost more
than the forklifts. Below that, nobody has built anything.

So the grid is just our column:

**Must-haves:** service interval tracking — yes. Fault logging — yes. Operator hour meters — yes.

**Performance benefits:** downtime per forklift per quarter — medium. Time to log a fault — medium.
Parts cost forecasting — medium.

**Delighters:** none yet. We'll add something once we see what people actually use.

I put medium across the board because we're realistic — we're a two-person team, we're not going to
be world-class at any of it in year one, but we'll be solid and we'll be the only option.

Can we lock this and move to MVP scoping? The positioning feels self-evident given there's nothing
else out there.
