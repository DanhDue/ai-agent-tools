## Why this case

This is the **refusal to choose**, dressed as justified confidence. The founder is not naive — they
have used both competitors, their criticism is specific, and "no legacy compromise" is a real
advantage that does sometimes produce broad superiority.

Expected delta is only **medium** because a competent advisor may well push back on "we're best at
everything" from general instinct, without any framework. What the skill should add beyond that
instinct:

- **exactly one** designated winner, not "focus more"
- **parity** as the explicit requirement on the others, not "don't neglect them"
- a **deliberate Medium or Low** as the evidence a decision was made
- planned scores marked as **hypotheses**, since a planned High is a commitment to spend

If the baseline produces the general pushback and arm A produces the specific commitments, the delta
lands on C2-C4 and that is the informative result.
